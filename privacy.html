Chat Dungeon

Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026. This covers chatdungeon.io.

What we collect

  • The name you pick and the messages you send. They're shown to everyone in the room and on stream, and read by the game so it can build what you asked for. The things the game builds from them can be saved and reused in later games.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address. It's used to rate-limit chat and to keep track of your energy pips and pledges. Your IP address itself isn't stored.
  • Your browser's local storage. It remembers your name and whether you hid the stream, on your own device. You can clear it at any time in your browser.

We don't ask for an account, an email address or any payment details.

Where it's stored

Chat messages, names, pips and pledges are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. They're used only to run the game and the chat room. We don't sell them.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on those visits.

  • You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

The Twitch stream

The stream is Twitch's own embedded player. Twitch may set cookies and collect data when it loads, under the Twitch Privacy Notice. You can hide the stream with the screen button in the room.

Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them (Google Privacy Policy).

Children

Chat Dungeon isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. For questions, or to ask us to remove messages you sent, email mottafier@gmail.com.
