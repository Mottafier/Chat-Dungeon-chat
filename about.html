Chat Dungeon

About Chat Dungeon

Chat Dungeon is a game you play together with a live stream. The streamer fights through a dungeon, and the people watching decide what's in it. Anything chat types can come to life: a knight made of spoons, a dragon made of pancakes, a skeleton DJ. A few seconds later it's built and attacking the player on stream.

The game is a work in progress, made by the streamer Mott. Things change often, and some features are still being tried out.

How to play

  1. Open chatdungeon.io while the streamer is live, pick a name and join the room. No account and no app needed.
  2. Type an idea in chat. It can be an enemy, a weapon, armor, a potion, a bomb, or just a message to everyone else.
  3. Each line gets a small icon showing what the game made of it: an enemy, a weapon, plain chat, and so on. When something you asked for appears in the game, your line lights up.
  4. Watch the stream next to the chat to see it happen.

Energy and pledges

Enemies are the big moments, so chat decides together which ones get made. Everyone has 5 energy pips that refill on their own over time. Every enemy idea in chat has a lightning button. Tap it to put one of your pips into that idea, whether it's yours or someone else's.

  • An idea with 3 pips comes to life next. The strongest ones need 5.
  • Each pip you put in moves that idea to the bottom of chat, so everyone can see it and add theirs.
  • Pips you spend are gone, even if the idea never gets enough. Ideas close after 10 minutes.
  • When you run low, the +2 button next to your pips gives you more, a few times an hour.

Weapons, items and plain chat

Weapons, armor and items cost nothing. The game reads every line and keeps item ideas for the streamer's shop. Plain chat is just chat, so say hi.

Be decent

What you type ends up in a live game in front of an audience. Keep it fun. Hateful, sexual or harassing messages may be removed, and the people who send them may be blocked. Chat is rate limited, so flooding doesn't help.
