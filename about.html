Chat Dungeon

About Chat Dungeon

Chat Dungeon is an interactive game played alongside live streams. Viewers join a streamer's room on this Site and chat during the stream, and what chat types can appear in the game as enemies, weapons and other surprises for the player.

No account or download is required. Pick a display name, join the room and start chatting.

Chat Dungeon is made and operated by Mottafier. It is in active development, and features may change.

Community Rules

Messages may be shown publicly on stream. Keep chat respectful. Hateful, sexual, harassing or otherwise inappropriate content is not allowed and may be removed, and users who post it may be blocked.
